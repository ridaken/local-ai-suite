# Research selection evaluation: pubmed-late-window-thrombectomy

**Question:** In adults with anterior-circulation acute ischemic stroke from large-vessel occlusion who were last known well 6–24 hours earlier, which pivotal randomized controlled trials established whether imaging-selected mechanical thrombectomy improves 90-day functional outcomes compared with medical therapy alone, and what did they find?
